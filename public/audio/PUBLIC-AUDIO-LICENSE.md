# Public audio license — Lyncr hold music

Bundled files under `public/audio/hold-*.wav` (including `hold-music.wav` and `hold-calm-short.wav`) are **original Lyncr-authored instrumental loops**, synthesized for classic US call-center / telephone hold (soft Rhodes-style chords, light bass, sparse guitar-ish lead, brushed ride).

| File | Preset | Style |
|------|--------|--------|
| `hold-calm.wav` / `hold-music.wav` | Classic hold (default) | Slow Fmaj7–Dm7–Gm7–C7 “bank / doctor office” Muzak |
| `hold-calm-short.wav` | Inline low-latency | Same Classic loop, ~7.5s for Telnyx `playback_content` |
| `hold-upbeat.wav` | Bright hold | Slightly brighter Cmaj7 lounge jazz |
| `hold-minimal.wav` | Soft hold | Quieter sparse Am9 pad |
